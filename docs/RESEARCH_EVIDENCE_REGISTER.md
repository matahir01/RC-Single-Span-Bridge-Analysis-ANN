## Release gates

- **Deterministic software verification:** repository V1 evidence applies to its documented BS EN and separate BS 5400/BD 37 scope. It is not an approval of a real bridge.
- **Probability-model adoption:** choose the production data/priors, DL categories, traffic scenario, dependence, acceptance criterion, and code/National Annex basis explicitly. The example configuration remains `assumptions_confirmed=false`.
- **Numerical research validation:** only after that choice, freeze seeds/configuration, publish dataset and split hashes, convergence, held-out and fresh near-limit metrics, FORM/direct-MC/ANN-MC comparison, and direct optimum recheck. Mark results exploratory if based on a proxy.
- **Project design approval:** requires client/authority criteria, site-specific actions, detailing, independent review and applicable National Annex; no software or thesis result substitutes for that process.
